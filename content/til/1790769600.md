[django-ox](https://oxpull.com/django-ox/) makes the database the Django task queue: transactional enqueue inside `atomic()`, no Redis/RabbitMQ, retries, worker-death recovery, recurring schedules, admin and metrics. Adoption read: pilot it for ordinary Django jobs; keep Celery for broker-scale workflows.
