[Armin Ronacher's subagent extension](https://github.com/mitsuhiko/agent-stuff/blob/main/extensions/subagent.ts) runs one observable Pi child at a time in tmux. The parent waits for the result, while I can attach to inspect or intervene. Worth preserving when adding hierarchy: synchronous coordination must not mean invisible execution.
