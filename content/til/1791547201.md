[Arnav Gupta's pi-subagent-manager](https://github.com/championswimmer/pi-subagent-manager) makes context inheritance explicit. Nested threads receive their parent's snapshot; independent roots start without its history. Threads stay around for steering and resume. I want synchronous coordination by default, forks for continuity and fresh context for independent review.
