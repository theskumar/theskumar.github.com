I've been writing code since school. In college I built conference websites and designed and built my university's website on Joomla. For the past fourteen years I've built backends, mostly in Python and Django: patient portals, a learning platform for Apple's retail staff, grant platforms for nonprofits, and subscription and media apps. Most of it was backend architecture, infrastructure, and technical leadership.

These days I lead engineering on [Rosey](https://meetrosey.ai), [Fueled](https://fueled.com)'s AI-native delivery platform, and help Fueled's teams and clients put AI agents to work.

More of my time now goes to research and product questions: how agents remember, retrieve, and get checked; how to build evals that catch real failures; and, as code gets cheaper to write, how to decide what's worth building and how to tell it worked. I'm interested in what helps in practice, and what still needs a human to check it.

I've written open source since 2013. I started [python-dotenv](https://github.com/theskumar/python-dotenv) in 2014 after a credential leak at work. I also helped maintain [cookiecutter-django](https://github.com/cookiecutter/cookiecutter-django), and I revived the [PyDelhi](https://pydelhi.org) meetup and ran it from 2014 to 2018.

I also build tools for how I work, like [Pi Pocket Blackhole](https://github.com/theskumar/pi-pocket-blackhole) for long agent sessions, and a knowledge base that agents keep up to date. [Projects](/projects/) has the full list, and my [TIL notes](/til/) catch what I learn along the way.

Off the clock, I'm on the outskirts of Delhi with my wife, my son Amay, and our dog [Hachi](https://instagram.com/hachi.softysingh), who all do a fine job of keeping me away from the keyboard.
