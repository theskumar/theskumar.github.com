__"Apps I’ve been using regularly this year.”__ Heavily inspired by [Jeff
Triplett](https://micro.webology.dev/2025/10/29/default-apps-here-are-my/).
Catalogued by Robb Knight’s [App Defaults](https://defaults.rknight.me/)

-  **Mail Client**: [Edison Mail](https://www.edisonmail.com/) and gmail in browser
-  **Notes**: [Obsidian], SimpleNotes
- **To-Do**: [Bullet Journel](https://bulletjournal.com/)
- **Photo Shooting**: iPhone, Canon 6D
- **Photo Editing**: PhotoShop, Gimp
- **Calendar**: [Notion Calendar](https://www.notion.com/product/calendar)
- **Cloud File Storage**: iCloud + Google Drive
- **RSS**: [NetNewsWire]
- **Contacts**: Apple Contacts & Google Contacts
- **Browser**: Arc, Zen Browser and Safari
- **Chat**: Apple Messages, Discord, Slack
- **Bookmarks**: RainDrop.io
- **Read It Later**: Safari Reading List
- **Word Processing**: Apple Pages
- **Spreadsheets**: Google Sheets
- **Presentations**: Figma
- **Shopping Lists**: Amazon
- **Budgeting and Personal Finance**: paisa
- **News**: Perplexity.ai, [NetNewsWire]
- **Music**: Youtube Music
- **Password Management**: 1Password and Apple Passwords
- **Code Editor**: [Zed] & Cursor
- **VPN**: [AdGuard], Zscalar
